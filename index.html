The Shop

a small room,
a lot of light.

A glass room down a quiet soi in Bang Rak — white walls, wooden benches, and a bakery case that is fullest first thing in the morning. There is not much seating. Most people take it away.

Single origins across light, medium and dark roasts, pulled the same way every morning from half past six. Nothing about it is complicated, which is rather the point.

There is usually a calico asleep somewhere. She was here first.
